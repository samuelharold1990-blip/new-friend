# Her reference photos

Put 1–3 clear face shots of her in this folder (png/jpg/webp). When Stable
Diffusion generation runs through ComfyUI with the
[IPAdapter Plus](https://github.com/cubiq/ComfyUI_IPAdapter_plus) custom nodes
installed, every selfie she sends is conditioned on the first reference image
here, so her face stays consistent across moods, outfits and settings.

How to create them (she's fictional — you generate her once):

1. In your SD UI, generate portraits with your chosen checkpoint until you find
   *her* — the face you want. Use a detailed appearance prompt and try seeds.
2. Save your favorite 1–3 shots here (a clean front-facing one works best).
3. That's it — the app picks them up automatically (Settings shows a
   "reference photo(s)" count when connected).

These images are never sent in chat; the chat photo pack lives in the mood
folders next to this one.
